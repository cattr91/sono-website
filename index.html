People, planet, profit

Data centres are growing faster than the people who can run them

Sono is the AI-enabled operating system that does the continuous watching, tracing and ranking, so the people you have can act faster and the equipment does more with less.

People
Clear tasks for every shift
Sono does the continuous watching and ordering of alarms. A short list with origin and context attached lowers the qualification needed on shift and lets you scale operations without scaling headcount at the same rate.
Planet
The right site, the right action
Choosing a site on grid, climate and water, and acting on each alarm sooner, cuts wasted energy and avoids emergency responses. A lower carbon footprint is the aim, and we will quantify it in the pilot.
Profit
Fewer outages, better capital decisions
SLA response times are met, affected customers hear from you first, and capital goes to sites that hold up over the life of the asset.
